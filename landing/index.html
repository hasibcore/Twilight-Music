Quick Setup

Installation Guides

Ready to run in seconds. Follow these simple steps for your platform.

Android

How to install on Android

  1. Download: Tap the Download APK (v3.6.1) button or scan the QR code from your phone.
  2. Open: Tap the downloaded app-release.apk file in your Downloads.
  3. Permit: If prompted with "Install unknown apps", enable Allow from this source.
  4. Launch: Tap Install, open Twilight, and begin listening with zero ads!
Windows PC

How to run on Windows 10 & 11

  1. Download ZIP: Click Download Windows (x64 ZIP).
  2. Extract: Right-click the downloaded .zip file and choose Extract All...
  3. Run: Open the extracted folder and double-click melody_tube.exe.
  4. SmartScreen: If Windows Defender displays "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway.
iOS / iPhone

How to install on iOS

  1. Instant PWA (Recommended): Open this page in Safari, tap the Share button (square with arrow) → select Add to Home Screen.
  2. Sideloading (AltStore): Download Twilight-iOS-v2.4.0.ipa, open AltStore on your phone, tap (+), and install the IPA.
  3. Sideloadly / TrollStore: Connect iPhone via USB, drag the IPA into Sideloadly, enter your Apple ID, and click Start.
Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Twilight Music really free with zero advertisements?
Yes! Twilight Music is 100% free and open-source under the MIT license. There are no subscriptions, no ads, and no premium paywalls. Audio streams are direct and unthrottled.
Where can I download the Android APK?
You can download the compiled app-release.apk directly from the Android card on this page or from the official GitHub Releases. You can also scan the QR code to download it directly onto your phone.
How do I run Twilight on Windows PC?
Download the Windows x64 ZIP package, extract it to any folder, and double-click melody_tube.exe. It is a portable build that does not modify your registry or require administrative rights.
Can I sync my playlists and favorites between devices?
Yes! Twilight includes Firebase cloud synchronization. Simply register with your email or Google account, and your saved tracks, custom playlists, and history will sync across all your phones and computers.
Where is the GitHub repository and source code?
The complete source code is available at https://github.com/hasibcore/Twilight-Music. You can clone it, submit issues, or fork it anytime!
